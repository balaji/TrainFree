### Full Description (1,980 / 4,000 characters)
```text
Train Free is a fast, 100% offline gym workout logger built to stay out of your way between sets. Pick an exercise or program, log weight and reps in seconds, and get back to the bar — with no account, no sign-in, no ads, and no subscriptions.

FAST MID-WORKOUT LOGGING
• See your previous weight and reps right next to every set
• Support for strength, duration, and distance exercises, plus warm-up and drop sets
• Built-in barbell plate calculator for quick loading
• Live rest timer with countdown notifications and custom alert chimes

PROGRAMS & EXERCISE CATALOG
• Start immediately with built-in sample programs (3-Day Full Body, 4-Day Upper/Lower, 5-Day Push Pull Legs) or build your own routines
• Browse the built-in exercise library, filter by muscle group or equipment, or add your own custom exercises

DETERMINISTIC PROGRESS & CALCULATORS
• Track weekly training volume, muscle-group balance, body weight, and consistency streaks
• Inspect per-exercise 1RM and volume progression charts over time
• Built-in 1RM Calculator comparing four proven formulas: Epley, Brzycki, Lombardi, and O'Conner
• Built-in BMI Calculator supporting Standard and Trefethen formulas with WHO International and Asia-Pacific classifications

CALENDAR & TRAINING JOURNAL
• Browse your full workout history on a clean calendar
• Write daily session notes in Markdown with live preview

HEALTH CONNECT & ON-DEVICE AI (OPTIONAL)
• Health Connect: Optionally write completed strength workouts and sync body weight two-way with smart scales and other health apps
• On-Device AI Coach & Form Tips: On supported Android devices with Gemini Nano, ask questions about your training log or view exercise form tips locally — everything works completely without AI on other devices

YOUR DATA BELONGS TO YOU
• Stored locally on your phone in a SQLite database
• Export and import full backups anytime — no cloud lock-in
• Supports kg/km and lb/mi units, Light/Dark/System themes, and English & Swedish
```
